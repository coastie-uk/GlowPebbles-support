Help

A few useful answers

How do I open the parent controls?

Hold one finger in each top corner for three continuous seconds. Moving or lifting either finger cancels the gesture.

How do I leave the game?

Open the parent controls, then hold Hold to exit for two seconds. If screen pinning is active, Android may also ask for your usual unpin action or device PIN.

Why does Android ask about screen pinning?

Screen pinning helps keep GlowPebbles in front during play. Android controls how pinning is enabled and disabled; the wording varies by device.

The animation feels slow. What can I try?

In parent controls, open Device & About and select the Low performance preset. Slow ripples and simpler shape sets can also help older hardware.

Which Android versions are supported?

GlowPebbles supports Android 5.1 (API 22) and newer. The current release is version 1.2.0.

Privacy

Nothing leaves the app.

GlowPebbles does not request Android permissions, connect to the Internet, show adverts, create user accounts, or include analytics. Settings and the lifetime tap count stay on the device.

Visiting this support site is separate from using the app. This site is hosted by GitHub Pages and is subject to GitHub’s privacy statement.
