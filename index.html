Tap, trail and glow.

GlowPebbles turns every touch into gentle colour, movement and sound. It is made for calm, shared play—and works entirely offline.

Ways to play

A different kind of calm for every moment.

Visual play styles and sound styles can be combined, so caregivers can find a gentle setup that suits the child and device.

01

Basic

Soft fading trails without temporary shapes—simple, colourful and uncluttered.

02

Shapes

Bubbles, hearts, smiles, stars, letters and numbers bloom around each touch.

03

Slow ripples

Gentle expanding rings replace trails and shapes for a quieter visual experience.

04

Drawing

Every active finger paints a persistent trail. A caregiver-only hold clears the canvas.

Sound

Chimes, nursery rhymes and Piano

Choose gentle random chimes, familiar nursery-rhyme melodies, or a full-screen C4–C5 Piano keyboard. Piano works over every visual play style, supports swipes and five-finger chords, and remains visible when sound is muted.

Install by sideloading

How to install the APK

  1. Download the APK on the Android device.
  2. Open the downloaded file from your browser or Files app.
  3. If Android blocks it, open the prompt's settings and allow Install unknown apps for that browser or Files app.
  4. Return to the APK and choose Install.
  5. Open GlowPebbles and review Android's screen-pinning prompt.

GlowPebbles is not currently distributed through an app store, so sideloading is required. Android's wording varies by device. You can turn off the browser or Files app's installation permission again after installing.

Help

A few useful answers

How do I open caregiver Settings?

Hold one finger in each top corner for three continuous seconds. Moving or lifting either finger cancels the gesture.

How do I leave the game?

Open Settings, then hold Hold to exit for two seconds. If screen pinning is active, Android may also ask for your usual unpin action or device PIN.

How does Piano work?

Choose Piano under sound style. It adds a full-screen keyboard over Basic, Shapes, Slow ripples, or Drawing. Tap keys, slide between them, or play up to five notes together.

Why does Android ask about screen pinning?

Screen pinning helps keep GlowPebbles in front during play. Android controls how pinning is enabled and disabled; the wording varies by device.

The animation feels slow. What can I try?

In Settings, open Device & About and select the Low performance preset. Basic and Slow ripples are also inexpensive choices for older hardware.

Which Android versions are supported?

GlowPebbles supports Android 5.1 (API 22) and newer. The current public download is the 1.3.0-RC1 release candidate; version 1.2.0 remains the stable packaged build.

Privacy

Nothing leaves the app.

GlowPebbles does not request Android permissions, connect to the Internet, show adverts, create user accounts, or include analytics. Settings and the lifetime tap count stay on the device.
